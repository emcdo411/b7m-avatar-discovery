John Butler is an award-winning broadcaster, SMU MLA graduate, and former personal assistant to the co-founder of Holiday Inns. He understands storytelling, scale, and high-value relationships.

## Sprint Architecture

### Phase 1 Pipeline — Rendered Content (Sprint Focus)

```
Equestrian / Travel RSS Feed + Event Calendar
                    ↓
        Script Generator (LLM + RAG Layer)
                    ↓
        Quality Gate (Editorial Voice Filter)
                    ↓
        ElevenLabs — John Butler Voice Clone
                    ↓
        HeyGen — Avatar Renderer + Brand Frame
                    ↓
    Finished Video Output (MP4 / Web-Ready)
                    ↓
  Distribution Agent → KWJB / YouTube / Social
                    ↓
        Analytics Dashboard + Feedback Loop
```

### Phase 2 Pipeline — Conversational Layer (Month 4+)

```
Website Visitor / Listener (WebRTC / STT)
                    ↓
  LangGraph Router Agent (Memory + RAG + Tools)
  ├── Equestrian Event Schedule
  ├── Station Programming Data
  └── Sponsor / Partner Info
                    ↓
        Response Planner + Personalization
                    ↓
ElevenLabs TTS → Tavus Phoenix Real-Time Avatar
                    ↓
    Stream → Audit Log → Content Intelligence
```

## Governance Framework

![Consent](https://img.shields.io/badge/Governance-Consent%20Required-FF4757?style=for-the-badge&logo=shield&logoColor=white)
![Audit](https://img.shields.io/badge/Governance-Full%20Audit%20Trail-D4A853?style=for-the-badge&logo=log&logoColor=white)
![DACR](https://img.shields.io/badge/IP%20Protection-DACR%20License%20v2.7-9B59B6?style=for-the-badge&logo=lock&logoColor=white)

> Your credibility as a broadcaster took 15 years to build. Every piece of AI content produced under this engagement is traceable, auditable, and protected.

### Pre-Production (Required Before Day 1 Builds Begin)

- **Likeness Rights Agreement** — scope limited to Butler7Media content, 12-month renewable, US territory
- **Voice Clone Consent Form** — explicit written permission for ElevenLabs model training on John Butler's voice
- **DACR License v2.7 carve-out** — pipeline architecture IP belongs to Epoch Frameworks LLC; all avatar outputs and produced content belong to Butler7Media

### In-Production (Ongoing)

- AI-generated content watermarking on all video outputs (FTC alignment)
- Full audit log: every generation timestamped with prompt, output, and approver
- Editorial quality gate: no video distributed without human review and approval
- Bias review on voice model for tonal drift across emotional variants

### Post-Production

- Training data retention and deletion policy executed per Butler7Media instruction
- Incident response protocol documented for any misuse or deepfake scenario
- Quarterly identity consistency review to prevent avatar drift over time

## Phase 2 Roadmap

![Phase 2](https://img.shields.io/badge/Phase%202-Conversational%20Avatar%20Agent-9B59B6?style=for-the-badge)
![Timeline](https://img.shields.io/badge/Timeline-Month%204%2B-3498db?style=for-the-badge)

Phase 2 converts the rendered pipeline into a live, interactive experience:

**Visitor asks the avatar a question** → LangGraph agent retrieves live equestrian schedules, sponsor info, and station programming → Avatar responds in John Butler's voice in real-time → Every interaction logged for content intelligence

**What content intelligence unlocks**: You start to know what your audience is actually asking about before you produce the next episode. The avatar becomes a discovery engine, not just a content delivery layer.

**Phase 3 Vision (Month 9+)**: The Butler7Media pipeline becomes a white-label proof-of-concept for other small broadcasters. Audacy sees it. The conversation with Scott Long becomes a larger agency play. Butler7Media is the reference client that opens the door.

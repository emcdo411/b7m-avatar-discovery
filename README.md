![Station](https://img.shields.io/badge/KWJB%20Canton-Active%20Radio%20Station-3498db?style=flat-square)

![Verticals](https://img.shields.io/badge/Verticals-Equestrian%20%7C%20Travel%20%7C%20Documentary-2ED573?style=flat-square)

Butler7Media is a Richardson, TX media operation with a radio station (KWJB Canton), documentary production capability, a travel content brand, and an owner who has publicly signaled interest in AI avatar and equestrian AI applications.

John W. Butler is an award-winning broadcaster, SMU MLA graduate, and former personal assistant to the co-founder of Holiday Inns. He has spent 15 years building credibility, relationships, and an editorial voice that audiences trust.

**The constraint he faces today**: Content production runs at human speed. John must be present for every recording, every narration, every segment. A 3-person operation has a ceiling.

**The thesis of this sprint**: His voice and his presence are already the brand. The sprint proves we can make them available at scale — for equestrian coverage, travel narration, and KWJB segments — without him recording every line.

> Butler7Media does not need more tools. It needs a scalable content architecture built around the identity it already owns.

## Sprint Architecture

### Phase 1 — Rendered Content Pipeline *(Sprint Focus)*

```
Equestrian / Travel RSS Feed + Event Calendar
                    ↓
        Script Generator (LLM + RAG Layer)
          [Editorial Voice Filter Applied]
                    ↓
        ElevenLabs — John Butler Voice Clone
                    ↓
        HeyGen — Avatar Renderer + Brand Frame
          [Station ID · Logo · Color Palette]
                    ↓
    Finished Video Output (MP4 / Web-Ready)
                    ↓
  Distribution Agent → KWJB / YouTube / Social
                    ↓
    Analytics Dashboard + Content Intelligence
```

### Phase 2 — Conversational Layer *(Month 4+)*

```
Website Visitor / Listener (WebRTC / STT)
                    ↓
  LangGraph Router Agent (Memory + RAG + Tools)
  ├── Equestrian Event Schedule
  ├── Station Programming Data
  └── Sponsor / Partner Info
                    ↓
        Response Planner + Personalization
                    ↓
ElevenLabs TTS → Tavus Phoenix Real-Time Avatar
                    ↓
    Stream → Audit Log → Content Intelligence
```

## Governance Framework

![Consent](https://img.shields.io/badge/Governance-Explicit%20Consent%20Required-FF4757?style=flat-square&logo=shield&logoColor=white)
![Audit](https://img.shields.io/badge/Governance-Full%20Audit%20Trail-D4A853?style=flat-square)
![Watermark](https://img.shields.io/badge/Compliance-AI%20Content%20Watermarking-3498db?style=flat-square)
![DACR](https://img.shields.io/badge/IP-DACR%20License%20v2.7-9B59B6?style=flat-square&logo=lock&logoColor=white)

> Your credibility as a broadcaster took 15 years to build. Every piece of AI content produced under this engagement is traceable, auditable, and protected. Your name is on it — it stays clean.

### Pre-Production (Required Before Day 1 Builds Begin)

- **Likeness Rights Agreement** — scope limited to Butler7Media content, 12-month renewable, US territory
- **Voice Clone Consent Form** — explicit written permission for ElevenLabs model training on John Butler's voice
- **DACR License v2.7 carve-out** — pipeline architecture IP belongs to Epoch Frameworks LLC; all avatar outputs and produced content belong to Butler7Media

### In-Production (Ongoing Through Sprint)

- AI-generated content watermarking on all video outputs (FTC alignment)
- Full audit log: every generation timestamped with prompt, output, and approver
- Editorial quality gate: no video distributed without human review and approval
- Bias review protocol for voice model tonal drift across emotional variants

### Post-Production

- Training data retention and deletion policy executed per Butler7Media instruction
- Incident response protocol documented for any misuse scenario
- Quarterly identity consistency review scheduled to prevent avatar drift over time
